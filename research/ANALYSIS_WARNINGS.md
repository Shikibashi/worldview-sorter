# Analysis warnings

- This is a voluntary, consent-selected application sample. Do not infer population prevalence or causal effects from its distributions. Unknown recruitment sources, interest in philosophy or quizzes, device access, route choice, completion burden, sharing, and repeat use may shape who appears.
- The authored construct registry, interpretation rules, thresholds, and affinity criteria are hypotheses and product semantics. Do not use them as observed traits or ground-truth ideology labels.
- Treat `branch_not_shown`, `not_reached`, `no_view`, `not_understood`, `not_applicable`, and substantive neutral answers as different states. Route- or branch-planned missingness is not ordinary nonresponse. The store rejects invalid response payloads, so no exported `invalid` category exists; technical presentation failures are not separately recorded.
- Do not pool item IDs across revisions without inspecting exact wording and administration context. The active questionnaire uses one fixed wording set; its historical presentation metadata do not establish alternate-form equivalence.
- Some repeats are linked by optional consent; other repeat users may look like distinct pseudonyms. Do not count administrations as independent people by default, and do not call a response difference a worldview change before checking date and model/version differences.
- `contributedDate` is a coarse submission date. Answer timing and response speeds were intentionally excluded; implausible-speed flags cannot be computed from this release. Unknown bots, internal testing, and incidents cannot be ruled out without operator records.
- `derived.ndjson` is reproducible application output. A successful replay proves export completeness only. Correlations with its own input answers cannot establish construct validity, rule accuracy, or philosophical identity.
- `authored-claim-index.json` is a view of the project's authored hypotheses. A legacy scope is not an independently reviewed exact proposition; a topic-level citation or broad source-record claim is not proof that the source supports every answer-to-rule mapping. Inspect sources and neighboring views directly.
- The copied replay engine is hashed at extraction. Check `snapshot.engineReplay.historicalSourceEquality` before treating it as historical production code: release 1.0.0 did not pin executable source, while later releases may. Even a matching archive is a technical replay guarantee, not scientific validation.
- A rare combination of answers and a linked repeat history can remain recognizable. Keep row-level data under the stated access controls, avoid unnecessary joins, and do not attempt re-identification.
- A withdrawal after extraction requires an operator-managed notice to recipients. Published copies cannot be automatically recalled. Never silently mutate this release; create a corrected snapshot.
