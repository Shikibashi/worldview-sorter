Choose a 64-question introduction, a 120-question Standard route, or the full 238-question pilot. Shorter routes leave more philosophical distinctions unmeasured. Research-informed, not a psychological diagnosis

The concepts and comparisons draw on academic philosophy and psychology. Our original questions and interpretation rules have not been empirically validated as an assessment. Your summary links the answers and sources behind each pattern. We do not assign a single ideology or a percentage match.

Answers stay in this browser unless you export them or explicitly contribute a completed pilot attempt to future research. The quiz has no account, ad tracker or automatic answer submission. Product analytics availability is being checked. Your hosting provider still receives ordinary page requests. Local storage can be cleared by the browser; save a backup to keep your answers.
