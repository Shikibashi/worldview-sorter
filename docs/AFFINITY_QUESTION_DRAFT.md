# Affinity question draft 1 (unreleased)

The [joint draft-pool reconciliation](../data/reviews/draft-pool-reconciliation-v1.json) compares these 25 drafts and the 16 dimension drafts with the active 562-item bank. It identifies existing questions to reuse, specific near duplicates, and a small first set for formal source and evidence review. Its priorities do not activate any draft item.

The [51-position expansion review](../data/affinities/expansion-review-v1.json) is the scope. The [question-gap matrix](../data/affinities/question-gap-review-v1.json) records, for **every** position in that review, exact existing item revisions, reusable earlier drafts, proposed new revisions, and what remains unsettled. The [25 new candidate questions](../data/items/affinity-gap-draft-v1.json) are additional content, not a route or affinity release. They do not change any respondent result. The candidate bank remains 562 items; no historical revision was edited.

These questions ask about philosophical claims, never whether a respondent identifies with a named school. They were selected for a specific doctrinal contrast that existing content leaves indirect. An item target in the candidate format identifies the nearest authored construct for editorial navigation; it is **not** an approved interpretation rule or an assertion that the construct has been empirically established. The draft's option IDs are proposed answer meanings, not scoring keys. Any future production rule needs its own exact proposition, source-to-claim review, positive/opposed/mixed/missing behavior, independent evidence review, route release, and regression fixtures. A named tradition may require several separately supported commitments.

The [11 proposed academic source records](../data/affinities/question-sources-v1.json) capture exact limited claims for questions where the older ledger offered only a broad or measurement-focused reference. They are not in the active source ledger. Their IDs must be included in a reviewed versioned source-ledger release before any draft item using them enters a public bank. No source is presented as validating these original questions.

## Existing questions retained instead of paraphrased

The bank already asks about moral truth aptness and error/expressivist alternatives (MEI005, MEI017–019), naturalism versus supernatural ontology (OMI011), physicalism (OMI002, OMI012, OMI032), mind-independent ordinary objects (OMI106–108), repeatable universals (OMI103–105), legal validity (PLI071–074), desert after all future benefit is removed (PLI064), non-domination (PLI010, PLI057), and state abolition versus obligation skepticism (PLI115–117). Those need proposition, source-claim, rule, route, and false-positive review before any affinity promotion. More paraphrases would increase burden without addressing those problems.

OMI113 was rejected from this draft because OMI108 already asks the same object-dependence question. The earlier unreleased NEI121 and EPI118 remain separate historical drafts and are referenced in the gap matrix where relevant. EPI119 appears as an existing item reference there but is itself an unreleased earlier draft. This file does not duplicate them.

## Remaining coverage limits

This batch does not create a direct existentialist-family or secular-humanist classification. The former covers internally diverse accounts of freedom, situated existence, and authenticity; the latter is a positive ethical and civic movement, not atheism plus kindness. A short identity-style item would be easier to write than to defend. Classical liberalism and Objectivism likewise require combinations of commitments across domains and historical interpretation; no isolated market or rights question can settle them. Classical utilitarianism's specifically hedonistic version remains unmeasured. Kantian autonomy, distinct Stoic physics/practice, and different acquisition theories remain visible gaps. The full [gap matrix](../data/affinities/question-gap-review-v1.json) records these limits per entry.

The next editorial stage is to compare every draft with its listed existing neighbor, check reading comprehension and cultural portability, review exact academic claims in a new source snapshot, decide the minimum route placement, and only then propose versioned direct rules. That work is not implied by authoring the questions. The existing six catalog entries and all historical results remain unchanged.
