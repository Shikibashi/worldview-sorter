The item counts, vignettes, formats, and rule opportunities are reproducible content facts. Completion time and fatigue have not been measured with people. The route chooser therefore states question counts rather than an asserted number of minutes. The quick route uses proposition pairs and discriminating alternatives across every domain; Standard adds specific gaps such as external-world stance, intervention and miracles, human biological and environmental explanations, psychological continuity, group agency, and market coordination. Full adds further assessable distinctions such as polycentric order, consent-based authority, tradition as evidence, and additional social and political positions. The exact guaranteed rule and facet IDs are in the versioned definition. A short route does not inherit Full's content merely because the model contains it.

Illustrative Quick omissions at the two-unit rule standard include personal persistence after death, natural-world exhaustiveness, market coordination, and democratic authorization. Standard still cannot satisfy that standard for afterlife belief, natural-world exhaustiveness, or democratic authorization. Some of these gaps persist in Full; the comparison report lists exact IDs. These omissions limit philosophical affinities even when a respondent's answers overlap on another doctrine. For burden and ordering, Quick has no consecutive same-domain pair, Standard and Full have at most two, and no route places mirror or scenario-group siblings adjacent. These are authored readability checks, not measured fatigue outcomes.

Selection was reviewed against direct rule evidence units, domain spread, nearby positions, branch prerequisites, wording cautions in the pilot content review, and response format diversity. The routes are nested by exact item revision. Their displayed order uses the existing dependency-aware packet ordering; earlier Full releases separated `NEI123@1` from `NEI122@1`; later releases substituted `EPI122@1` for `EPI021@1` and `NEI121@1` for `NEI101@1`; route 2.3 substituted `EPI103@1` and `EPI123@1` for the former pragmatic-maxim pair; route 2.4 preserves those exact items; route 2.5 adds `NEI124@2` and `NEI132@1` only to Full; route 2.7 adds the scoped PL39 production-control evidence only to Full; route 2.8 adds `NEI134@1` and `NEI135@1` only to Full. Quick and Standard include one comparative ranking item as a research variable; it does not create a direct respondent interpretation. Longer routes add authored content, not a calibrated amount of certainty.
After Quick or Standard, a user can keep the current result, continue to a deeper route, or request up to six questions for a chosen domain. The existing follow-up planner is restricted to reviewed, not-yet-assigned current Full-route items and public direct rules. It prioritizes mixed, weak, insufficient, or unmeasured evidence, and uses directly mapped defining doctrine as an authored tie-breaker within an evidence state. A partial mapping is not promoted to a full doctrinal criterion, and no affinity hints appear while answering. It includes necessary branch prerequisites and records the reviewed pool, candidate questions, withheld candidates, prior rule states, selected item revisions, and reasons. A conditional item may be skipped if its prerequisite answer does not apply, so controls say "up to" the assigned count. Branch-skipped items do not count as available direct evidence: for example, Quick cannot assess the personal/agentic deity distinction when its conditional discriminator is skipped, though Full still has two unconditional discriminators. A domain with no justified next question offers none. Full has no remaining current-pilot clarification content; the interface does not manufacture a next question. Clarification is optional and can be repeated only while useful reviewed items remain.

Escalation appends only missing item revisions to the same administration. Answered items are retained. A Standard continuation after clarification may contain more than the base 120 items if the chosen clarification included Full-route items outside Standard. Each completed stage stores a raw-response checkpoint with its route, interpretation model, adaptive policy, and affinity catalog references. Saved selection events retain the actual item order and reasons, so reopening does not rerun today's adaptive policy to guess the old route. A fresh Full route uses the current versioned pilot form and remains eligible for the separately consented research workflow. A Quick or Standard route extended to Full has a different packet order and is not submitted as a frozen research pilot.

Affinity comparisons consume the interpreted propositions from the current assigned route. An unasked defining doctrine stays unmeasured; political overlap cannot fill a missing metaphysical or metaethical criterion. Multiple affinities or no sufficiently established affinity remain normal. Raw answers are never mapped directly to traditions. These authored route opportunities are not item information, IRT parameters, conditional standard errors, calibrated stopping rules, or evidence of an optimal length. Those decisions require representative response data, comprehension work, and empirical validation.
