**Historical milestone:** This page describes `pilot-candidate-1.0.0` and its original 141-rule model. The active [model release 1.5.0](../data/releases/model-release-v1.5.0.json) has a separate 240-item Full route and 141 public direct rules. The prior 238 revisions and SO09 retirement remain intact. See the [model changelog](MODEL_CHANGELOG.md) and [moral-scope review](governance/MORAL_SCOPE_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md).

`pilot-candidate-1.0.0` is a frozen content and interpretation candidate. Its manifest retains the historical status string `candidate_for_human_piloting`; that label records its original intended study stage, not a prerequisite for AI-led development or release. It is not a validated, calibrated, optimized, or reliable scale. The [manifest](../data/pilots/pilot-candidate-v1.json) records the exact 238 item IDs and revisions, domain counts, bank and registry versions, route and instrument versions, result semantics, authored evidence-unit thresholds, and source hashes. The [240-item review](../data/pilots/content-review-v1.json) gives a decision and rationale for every question selected by the previous full-route policy with seed `pilot-v1-content`.

Those numbers describe rule opportunities, not how many conclusions any respondent will receive or how many exact propositions have been independently reviewed. The retained gaps are substantive. ## Evidence semantics

These are editorial response patterns over exact questions, not probabilities or calibrated cutoffs.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Supported | At least the rule's minimum number of distinct authored units support its recorded target, with no opposing unit. For a legacy scope-only rule, that label is not a separately reviewed exact proposition. |
| Leaned toward | Some directional evidence exists, but fewer units than the authored support or opposition requirement. Direction remains explicit. |
| Mixed or context-dependent | At least one unit supports and another opposes; general-answer versus case divergence is identified when present. |
| Opposed | At least the rule's minimum number of distinct authored units oppose its recorded target, with no supporting unit; the same legacy-scope limitation applies. |
| Insufficient evidence | The frozen route contains enough appropriate directional content, but this respondent's answers do not justify a direction. Neutral, no view, uncomprehended, skipped, and missing answers are retained separately in provenance. |
| Not measured | The frozen route lacks enough distinct appropriate content for support **or** opposition. A lone relevant answer does not turn this into a respondent-level finding. |

The threshold is an authored duplicate-control rule. It is not an empirical independence, reliability, certainty, or confidence estimate. The result engine uses route item revisions to determine content availability before looking at answer direction. Direct answers are never filled from another construct or a derived family. Contradictory answers within a proposition become mixed. The only declared cross-proposition tension is support for miraculous action alongside opposition to supernatural reality; compatible biological and environmental causal beliefs are left compatible. The `RC11` conjunction requires both deity-existence and conditional agentic-divinity propositions to be supported. A conflicting direct divinity answer withholds the derived conclusion.

## Result hierarchy and boundaries

The public result is organized as worldview → repository domain → authored philosophical subfacet → exact proposition **or marked inherited rule scope** → state and explanation → inspectable rule, item revision, answer meaning, and academic source. `direct-presentation-1` records whether a public direct row has an exact proposition and a rule-linked supporting source claim, separately from its frozen evidence state. Scope-only rows use authored-pattern wording rather than an unqualified proposition claim. An exact proposition without a linked supporting claim explicitly says that its academic link is under review; a general source citation is not represented as support for that exact rule. These qualifications also travel with newly created share text. The 141 frozen public direct rules currently have no rule-linked supporting source claims, and 126 retain inherited scopes. This is a claim-traceability gap, not a finding that every underlying philosophical interpretation is false.

The frozen `RC11` derived rule can historically return `supported`, but its `divine-existence` prerequisite stores only a broad scope, and the derived rule and prerequisites lack rule-linked supporting source claims. The separate `derived-presentation-1` qualification therefore labels that synthesis as awaiting model review and retains the frozen engine state only in technical provenance. It is excluded from new share cards and resolved-pattern counts until a versioned philosophical review qualifies its exact prerequisites and source links. A source-claim link is a traceability prerequisite, not proof of philosophical or empirical validity. The overview is labeled **Selected answer patterns**: it selects direct rules with authored positive evidence, and each card discloses any proposition or source-link limitation. It does not call those patterns clearly evidenced philosophical commitments. The topic map retains opposed and mixed results. Mixed/insufficient and not-measured areas have separate sections. Research-tier rules and uncovered ontology entries do not become positive public claims. The 49-construct audit and retained coverage gaps describe content still requiring questions or conceptual work.

The internal [affinity stress examples](../data/pilots/affinity-stress-v1.json) remain as historical architecture checks. The separate [Philosophical Affinity Catalog 1.0.0](AFFINITY_CATALOG_V1.md) now adds six sourced, scoped comparisons over interpreted public propositions. It records overlap, divergence, contradictory or unresolved evidence, unmeasured defining doctrine, neighbors, and non-entailments. The catalog has its own version and byte-pinned manifest; it supplies no identity, winner, percentage, or distance.

## Replay and limits

A saved pilot session records the exact `instrumentVersion`, packet policy version, ordered item revisions, raw response states, and client version. The pilot result requires that frozen route and `generic-1.0.0-pilot`; a different model version cannot silently reinterpret the saved session. Later models can use the same raw responses only through an explicit versioned reinterpretation path. Older 240, 160, 120, and 80 forms remain at their versioned paths and are replayed by the compatibility suite.

Run `npm test` and `npm run test:quiz:browser` for software verification. Passing these checks establishes deterministic routing, state handling, provenance, compatibility, and browser rendering. AI semantic review, source comparison, and adversarial fixtures can support authored revisions without a human-interview gate. Claims about actual respondent comprehension, item dependence, subgroup behavior, reliability, or validity still need appropriate real-response evidence; optional cognitive interviews can inform that work. [Pölzler's analysis of measuring moral realism](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13164-018-0401-8) illustrates how neighboring philosophical positions and first-order prompts can confound inference; the [Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing](https://www.testingstandards.net/uploads/7/6/6/4/76643089/9780935302356.pdf) discuss evidence for intended interpretations. Neither source validates these original items.
