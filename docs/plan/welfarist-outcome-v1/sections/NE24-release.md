# NE24 outcome-value release

## Input

Active `model-release-1.16.0`, `NEI124@1` in the unreleased affinity-gap draft, current welfare/value and act-rightness items, and SEP entries on well-being, consequentialism, and environmental ethics.

## Output

An immutable successor with `NEI124@2`, `NEI132@1`, the scoped `NE24` proposition, source-to-claim links, and a Full-only evidence path. Quick and Standard retain their exact item revisions.

## Acceptance

The proposition concerns whether welfare exhausts ultimate outcome value in the stated contrasts. It does not entail utilitarianism, act-maximization, hedonism, a distribution rule, or that rights cannot constrain action. A denial of value in one unseen-beauty case alone cannot establish general welfarism. Missing and route-omitted evidence never become neutral or opposition. Historical releases remain reproducible.
