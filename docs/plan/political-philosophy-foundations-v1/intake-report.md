## 2. Philosophical Clusters & Doctrinal Boundaries

The 12 rules organize into five coherent philosophical clusters:

### Cluster 1: Liberty — Non-Interference vs. Non-Domination
- **`noninterference`** (`PL06`, headline tier): Liberal negative liberty as the absence of actual external interference (Isaiah Berlin, J.S. Mill).
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI007` (Likert, 17 words) and `PLI051` (Vignette choice, `PL-S04`, 28 words).
  - Directional contract: 2 support units, 2 oppose units.
- **`nondomination`** (`PL07`, primary tier): Republican liberty as the absence of arbitrary power or dependency, even if no actual interference occurs (Philip Pettit, Quentin Skinner).
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI010` (Likert, 22 words) and `PLI057` (Vignette choice, `PL-S08`, 26 words).
  - Directional contract: 2 support units, 2 oppose units.

### Cluster 2: Rights Grounding — Preinstitutional vs. Instrumental
- **`construct-PL27`** (`PL27`, primary tier): Preinstitutional natural rights grounding rights in human moral status prior to state conventions (John Locke, Robert Nozick).
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI100` (Likert, 18 words), `PLI101` (Likert, 16 words), and `PLI102` (Likert, 17 words).
  - Directional contract: 3 support units, 3 oppose units.
- **`construct-PL28`** (`PL28`, primary tier): Institutional and instrumental justification of rights grounded in social utility, rule stability, or protected interests (Jeremy Bentham, J.S. Mill, Joseph Raz).
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI103` (Likert, 20 words), `PLI104` (Likert, 19 words), and `PLI105` (Likert, 18 words).
  - Directional contract: 3 support units, 3 oppose units.

### Cluster 3: State Authority & Force Monopoly vs. Anarchism
- **`construct-PL29`** (`PL29`, primary tier): Territorial monopoly of legitimate physical force as a defining characteristic of political authority (Max Weber).
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI106` (Likert, 17 words), `PLI107` (Likert, 19 words), and `PLI108` (Likert, 16 words).
  - Directional contract: 3 support units, 3 oppose units.
- **`construct-PL32`** (`PL32`, primary tier): Institutional state-abolition preference and rejection of state legitimacy (Robert Paul Wolff, Pierre-Joseph Proudhon, Murray Rothbard).
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI115` (Likert, 14 words), `PLI116` (Likert, 13 words), and `PLI117` (Likert, 19 words).
  - Directional contract: 3 support units, 3 oppose units.
- **`ph-democratic-authorization`** (`PL04`, headline tier): Democratic authorization as a necessary condition for legitimate political rule.
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI006` (Likert, 25 words) and `PLI027` (Likert, 21 words).
  - Directional contract: 2 support units, 2 oppose units.

### Cluster 4: Theories of Justice & Punishment
- **`procedural-justice`** (`PL16`, primary tier): Procedural justice holding that outcomes are just if generated by fair procedures, regardless of substantive distribution (John Rawls, Robert Nozick).
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI015` (Likert, 21 words) and `PLI062` (Vignette choice, `PL-S10`, 29 words).
  - Directional contract: 2 support units, 2 oppose units.
- **`substantive-justice`** (`PL17`, primary tier): Substantive justice holding that justice is defined by the fairness or equality of final outcome distributions.
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI016` (Likert, 19 words) and `PLI063` (Vignette choice, `PL-S11`, 21 words).
  - Directional contract: 2 support units, 2 oppose units.
- **`ph-desert-punishment`** (`PL18`, primary tier): Retributive desert as the primary justification of legal punishment.
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI017` (Likert, 19 words) and `PLI064` (Vignette choice, `PL-S12`, 25 words).
  - Directional contract: 2 support units, 2 oppose units.

### Cluster 5: Political Community & Religious Norms
- **`ph-national-obligations`** (`PL23`, primary tier): Special moral and political obligations owed to fellow citizens or members of the nation.
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI021` (Likert, 20 words) and `PLI068` (Vignette choice, `PL-S15`, 25 words).
  - Directional contract: 2 support units, 2 oppose units.
- **`ph-religious-public-law`** (`PL26`, primary tier): Religious doctrine as an authoritative ground for public civil law and state policy.
  - Assigned candidate items: `PLI023` (Likert, 21 words) and `PLI070` (Vignette choice, `PL-S17`, 30 words).
  - Directional contract: 2 support units, 2 oppose units.
