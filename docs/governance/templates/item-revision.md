# Item revision proposal prompts

Copy `model-change.json`; use `objectType: item`, usually `changeClass: semantic_clarification` or `substantive_revision`.

- Quote the old and proposed stems/options; identify every changed quantifier and response meaning.
- Name the target proposition, nearest alternative, false-positive risk, and why a more general question is insufficient.
- For a policy example, justify the philosophical discriminator and jurisdiction limits; consider research-only use.
- Preserve the old revision. Check routes, rules, translations, response scale, historical fixtures, and export wording metadata.
