# Normative self-interest boundary, release 1.14.0

This is an authored content decision, not empirical item validation. The prior
[mapping review](SELF_INTEREST_MAPPING_REVIEW.md) and draft proposal
`MCP-2026-005` remain historical records. Release 1.13.0 and its model, item
revisions, routes, and catalog remain available for replay.

## Exact public proposition

`construct-NE15` now tests: “Each person morally ought to make their own
long-term good the ultimate end of their actions; another person’s need alone
supplies no independent moral reason.” This is a conjunction. Its first clause
asks for a universal moral end; its second distinguishes a need-based moral
reason from a reason arising only through the agent’s own good.

[SEP on egoism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/egoism/) distinguishes moral,
practical, and descriptive self-interest theses. Its standard ethical egoism
also requires the stronger claim that an act is right if and only if, and
because, it maximizes the agent’s self-interest. This questionnaire does not
establish that stronger condition. The vignette tests a conceptual distinction
from uncompensated other-regarding reasons; SEP does not validate the item’s
wording, independence, or threshold.

## Evidence and route

| Item | Directional contribution | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `NEI100@1` | Agreement supports the own-good end; disagreement opposes it. | This and `NEI101@1` form one authored unit because their semantic target is nearly the same. |
| `NEI101@1` | Same own-good end; retained in the bank for history and research. | No longer administered on the Full route; cannot supply a second corroborating unit. |
| `NEI121@1` | `own_good_only` supports denial of need-alone moral reason; `independent_duty` and `independent_reason` oppose it. | `context_matters` is nondirectional; one answer alone cannot establish the conjunction. |

The active Full route exchanges `NEI101@1` for `NEI121@1` at the same position.
Quick and Standard do not administer this pair, so `construct-NE15` is normally
`not_measured` there. On Full, agreement with `NEI100` and the exclusive
reason answer to `NEI121` supply two authored directions. Opposing answers
to both oppose the conjunction. Pulling in opposite directions produces
`mixed_context_dependent`; one direction leans, and nondirectional/missing
answers are insufficient. These are engineering evidence rules, not calibrated
psychometric thresholds.

`NEI102@1` remains in the historical bank but leaves the successor rule. Its
reverse-keyed disagreement could mean rejecting permissible self-concern or
rejecting the proposed ultimate standard; it does not uniquely support
exclusive normative self-interest.

The released fourth vignette option asks whether further case facts matter.
The unreleased draft instead offered an `undecided` option, which duplicated
the questionnaire's separate no-view state. This wording adjustment occurred
before the item entered a released bank and does not change historical results.

## Neighbors and non-entailments

The interpretation must survive duties of self-care plus independent duties to
others, agent-centered prerogatives plus non-obligatory reasons for helping,
rational egoism without a specifically moral requirement, psychological
egoism, and Stirnerian ownness. Agreement does not establish a maximizing
if-and-only-if account, actual motives, a view about welfare, exploitation,
market institutions, or Objectivism. Denying that *need alone* gives an
independent moral reason does not imply unwillingness to help or lack of
benevolence: assistance can be justified within a self-interest account.

[Rand’s primary ethics essay](https://courses.aynrand.org/works/the-objectivist-ethics/)
and [SEP’s Rand entry](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ayn-rand/) place
rational self-interest in a wider account of life, reason, and value. The
Objectivism catalog therefore retains rational self-interest as a defining
doctrine but treats this narrow `NE15` observation as **partial**. The catalog
does not infer Rand’s ethics from generic self-priority, nor a complete
tradition from this one result. The ethical-egoism catalog’s scoped criterion
tracks this exact narrow conjunction; its stronger rightness/maximization
criterion remains unmeasured.

## Release and remaining review

Release 1.14.0 has new bank, model, route, catalog, source ledger, and
release bindings. It does not rewrite raw responses. Historical 1.13.0
administrations use the old rule and catalog; a current reinterpretation is a
separate operation. `NEI121@1` is an original authored vignette derived from a
philosophical contrast, not copied wording.

The hypothetical instruction that helping would not advance one’s long-term
good and the reason/duty distinction need respondent comprehension work.
Independence of the two administered evidence units and real response patterns
require human data. They are limitations on future claims, not reasons to
silently restore the old near-paraphrase as corroboration.
