# Literal moral truth claims: scoped public interpretation

**Release target:** model 1.10.0, interpretation model `generic-1.7.0-pilot`. This review promotes one exact proposition already asked by the Full route. It adds no question or affinity entry and changes no historical answer mapping.

## Philosophical distinction and source role

[SEP, Moral Cognitivism vs. Non-Cognitivism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/moral-cognitivism/) distinguishes moral sentences being truth-apt and expressing beliefs from noncognitivist accounts of their function. Its discussion of cognitivist subjectivism, hybrid theories, quasi-realism, and minimalist truth cautions against treating ordinary truth-talk as a complete metaethical identity. [SEP, Moral Realism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/moral-realism/) separately requires that at least some moral claims are actually true; truth-aptness alone is compatible with error theory. These are conceptual sources, not validation of the original Worldview Sorter questions.

The released proposition is **“Some ordinary moral wrongness statements make claims that can literally be true or false.”** Its claim-level source is `sep-moral-truth-claim-criterion` in source ledger 0.11.0. The source supports the semantic distinction and the recorded caveats. It does not support an inference to moral realism, error theory, expressivism, or a theory of objective moral facts.

The mixed option is not automatic support because it leaves the specific combination unspecified. Two distinct directional units are required for `supported` or `opposed`; conflicting directions become `mixed_context_dependent`; one unit yields only `leaned_toward`. Asked but nondirectional evidence is `insufficient_evidence`, while omitted Quick and Standard content is `not_measured`.

## Neighboring views and false positives

- A moral error theorist can accept truth-aptness and deny that any substantive moral claim is true. This rule cannot establish moral realism.
- A cognitivist relativist can regard standards-dependent moral claims as truth-apt. This rule cannot establish stance-independent moral facts.
- A sophisticated expressivist may permit deflationary truth-talk while rejecting robust cognitivist semantics. The result is a reported answer pattern, not a settled academic classification.
- Generic moral universalism, objectivity, or strong disapproval does not supply either truth-claim evidence unit.
