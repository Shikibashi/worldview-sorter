# Pragmatism criterion: preliminary content review

Status: historical predecessor review. [Model release 1.15.0](PRAGMATISM_MAXIM_RELEASE.md) implements a narrower proposition and a new discriminator. This document describes the earlier evidence problem; its frozen-release statements refer to the state reviewed at the time.

## Exact dependency

The catalog's defining `pragmatism/pragmatic-maxim` criterion maps directly to public rule `construct-EP16`. The rule stores a broad `scope`, not an explicit standalone `proposition` or rule-linked source claim. Its three revision-1 mapped items all target `EP16` and each has a separate authored unit ID, but the frozen 238-item route administers only two:

| Item | Frozen route? | Target in the current wording | Supporting / opposing answer values | Review issue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `EPI103@1` | **No** | Clarify a disputed idea by possible differences in experience or action. | Agree `1,2` / disagree `-2,-1` | Closest direct statement of the maxim, but unavailable to this route. Agreement could also mean the method is useful without endorsing it as exhaustive. |
| `EPI104@1` | Yes | Question whether a dispute without conceivable consequential difference is substantive. | Agree `1,2` / disagree `-2,-1` | A qualified application of `EPI103`; the route has only this general-method positive item. |
| `EPI105@1` | Yes | Meaning can **always** be settled without any experiential or practical consequence. | Disagree `-2,-1` / agree `1,2` | Disagreement with the absolute reverse wording can arise in people who reject the full pragmatic maxim. It currently supplies the second supporting unit. |

The exact inference that could be reviewed is narrow: a respondent endorses using conceivable experiential or practical bearings to clarify disputed conceptions. That does not entail any specific pragmatist theory of truth, rejection of realism, atheism, or identification as a pragmatist. The catalog compares doctrine rather than assigning identity, but its defining overlap currently inherits the rule's scope text. A synthetic sensitivity fixture groups `EPI103` and `EPI104` as one authored content opportunity and treats both directions of the absolute `EPI105` as non-directional. Under that preview, the frozen route has only one direct content opportunity and leaves `EP16` not measured; the frozen model still reports its historical state. This test exposes a content-evidence gap rather than proving empirical dependence between the items.

## Academic basis and neighboring views

[Legg and Hookway's SEP account](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/pragmatism/) presents the pragmatic maxim as a method for clarifying concepts and apparently empty disputes, while describing substantial differences among pragmatists about truth and realism. It also cautions that Peirce's maxim is a clarification tool rather than his complete theory of meaning. [Peirce's 1878 text](https://www.peirce.org/writings/p119.html) treats conceivable practical effects as central to clarifying a conception. These sources support the doctrinal *distinction*; they do not validate these original questionnaire items or show that the three responses are independent evidence units.

Generic fallibilism, willingness to test claims in experience, and using consequences as one helpful clarification technique are neighboring positions. `EPI103` and `EPI104` bear on the method more directly than a generic fallibilism item, but the present answer pattern cannot by itself establish exclusive commitment to Peirce's full account of meaning. Disagreement with the absolute `EPI105` is especially vulnerable to that false positive. Conversely, a respondent could endorse the pragmatic method in a qualified form while resisting the categorical wording of `EPI104` or `EPI105`.

## Required decision before a new release

1. Review the exact rule proposition and whether its supporting and opposing answer meanings should be symmetric. Retain explicit non-entailments and source-to-claim text on the versioned rule.
2. Review `EPI103`/`EPI104` for semantic redundancy and local dependence, and `EPI105` for reverse wording and the force of “always.” Do not label them empirically dependent without response-process or response data.
3. Use cognitive interviews and, when consented data permit, item-pair and response-distribution analysis to examine how people understand the three items. This is empirical work still outstanding, not evidence to infer from the current authored unit IDs.
4. If the criterion or rule changes, submit a philosophical and engineering proposal, preserve the released model/catalog, version changed artifacts, and add positive, negative, mixed, missing, near-neighbor, and historical fixtures. Do not silently promote the inherited scope into an exact proposition.
