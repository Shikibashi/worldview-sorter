# Effective social control of production: content review

**Disposition:** approve one narrow, direct proposition for Full only. Do not infer a complete socialist tradition or expand the affinity catalog in this release.

## Exact proposition

> In the two stated political-economy settings, the respondent favors meaningful worker or democratically accountable public control over production rather than private owner/manager control or state control insulated from workers and the public.

The two settings are (1) binding worker participation in workplace decisions versus owner-appointed management, and (2) effective control over major productive enterprises by workers, an accountable wider public, unaccountable state officials, or private owners. The direct result reports this authored cross-setting preference only.

## Why the active public model did not measure it

The active model measured private-property claims (`PLI013`, `PLI054`), market versus deliberate coordination (`PLI035`, `PLI055`), and other political-economic values. Those do not tell us who should exercise effective control over productive enterprises. Common ownership of some resources (`PLI014`) is also insufficient: it does not establish a general preference about control of firms or production. The workplace-vote question (`PLI060@1`) appeared in the candidate bank but not the public Full route, and by itself would not measure the broader asset-level distinction.

`PLI060@2` preserves the existing wording and PL14 primary target while adding a secondary, direct PL39 evidence mapping. `PLI123@2` revises an unreleased affinity-gap draft into a plain-language item about major productive enterprises, adds a non-forced `other` response, and distinguishes accountable social control from unaccountable state administration. The registry's `candidateItemTarget` is 1 because it counts primary target items; the public rule separately requires both `PLI060@2` and `PLI123@2` before it reports supported or opposed. The questions are complementary: one concerns firm-level decision rights; the other concerns the ordinary control arrangement for major productive enterprises. Neither is treated as statistically independent.

## Academic basis and disagreement

- [SEP, “Socialism,” §§1 and 4.2](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/socialism/): its 2024 revision identifies social, democratic control over the bulk of the means of production as a minimum institutional distinction; separates that from unaccountable state power; and describes market-socialist and other institutional variants. This supports the scoped distinction, not a claim that all socialist traditions choose one governance form.
- [IEP, “Socialism,” §§1.a–1.b](https://iep.utm.edu/socialis/): distinguishes legal from effective ownership and private, state, and social control. It notes that socialists disagree about markets, planning, and institutional realization.

The relevant disagreement concerns how social control should be organized: worker self-management, democratically accountable public institutions, planning, and markets can vary. The item treats worker-governed and accountable-public arrangements as distinct answers within the broad social-control family. It does not infer that they are interchangeable in every context.

These sources support the philosophical distinction. They do not validate item wording, respondent comprehension, route placement, the two-unit evidence threshold, or psychometric properties.

## Items and directional meanings

| Item | Revision | Direct target | Supporting answers | Opposing answers |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `PLI060` | 2 | Workplace decision rights | `strong_a`, `lean_a` (binding decisions by participants) | `lean_b`, `strong_b` (owner-appointed management) |
| `PLI123` | 2 | Effective control of major productive enterprises | `worker_governed`, `social_governed` | `state_only`, `private_owners` |

The `mixed` and `other` PLI123 options are non-directional. Neutral, no-view, not-understood, unanswered, or otherwise non-directional evidence never supplies opposition. Two authored evidence units agreeing yield `supported` or `opposed`; opposing directions yield `mixed_context_dependent`; one directional unit yields `leaned_toward`; presented but nondirectional/missing responses yield `insufficient_evidence`; omitted route evidence yields `not_measured`.

## Neighbors, non-entailments, and false-positive controls

Important neighbors include common ownership legitimacy, workplace participation without social asset control, public ownership with democratic accountability, unaccountable state ownership, market coordination, planning, social insurance/redistribution, and democratic political authority. The items map directly to PL39; no conclusion is derived from PL12, PL13, PL14, welfare-state preference, or a tradition affinity.

The result does not entail socialism, democratic socialism, communism, anti-capitalism, abolition of personal property, collective ownership of all assets, a legal ownership title, central planning, market rejection, redistribution, political democracy in all domains, or state expansion/abolition. A respondent may favor markets while preferring worker or public control of production; the market question is a separate criterion.

Likely false positives include: supporting public services or redistribution; supporting commons in a limited resource case; preferring market coordination; favoring workplace votes while preferring private control over major assets; or supporting state ownership without public accountability. Only answers to the two exact items contribute. Workplace/asset disagreement is shown as mixed/context-dependent, not corrected as inconsistency.

## Route, affinity, and historical consequences

The exact two revisions are added to the Full successor only, reaching 249 questions. Quick and Standard preserve their existing item references and report `not_measured`. The affinity catalog advances only to pin the new model/instrument versions; no tradition definition or affinity criterion changes. Any future tradition comparison must be proposed separately and may use only this proposition as a scoped institutional criterion, retaining its other unmeasured doctrine.

Bank 0.18.0, PLI060@1, model 1.18.0, route 2.6.0, their manifests, and the unreleased PLI123@1 draft remain unchanged. The successor is a current interpretation, not a mutation of historical administrations.

This is authored, source-informed content, not a validated measure. Human response data and cognitive interviewing are needed to evaluate comprehension and whether the two settings behave as intended.
