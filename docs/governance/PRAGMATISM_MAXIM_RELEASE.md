# Pragmatic clarification method, release 1.15.0

This successor resolves the risk identified in the [predecessor review](PRAGMATISM_MAPPING_REVIEW.md). It is an authored content decision, not empirical validation. Release 1.14.0 and earlier items, routes, interpretations, affinities, and engine snapshots remain available for historical replay.

## Exact public proposition

The construct-EP16 rule now tests: “Disputed ideas should be clarified through their conceivable effects on experience or conduct; if two formulations have no conceivable difference in those effects, their apparent conceptual difference is not substantive.”

[Legg and Hookway's SEP account](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/pragmatism/) describes the pragmatic maxim as a method for clarifying concepts through conceivable practical bearings and diagnosing apparently empty disputes. [Peirce's 1878 essay](https://www.peirce.org/writings/p119.html) is the primary source for the maxim. The SEP distinguishes this method from a complete theory of meaning and records differences among pragmatists about truth and realism. These sources support the philosophical distinction; they do not validate the original questions or a two-unit inference threshold.

## Nearby views and limits

Generic fallibilism, scientific testing, and treating consequences as one helpful clue do not establish the stronger empty-dispute judgment. EPI123 makes the last distinction explicit. A logical verificationist might also endorse this scoped method; genuine overlap on one criterion is not identity with Pragmatism. Endorsement does not entail that usefulness makes a belief true, that all meaning reduces to observation, anti-realism, atheism, or a political view.

The versioned Pragmatism comparison retains the pragmatic method as a defining scoped criterion and links it to the exact EP16 proposition. Its characteristic fallibilism criterion remains nonexclusive; its truth-and-realism criterion remains disputed and unmeasured. Multiple tradition comparisons may overlap, and no percentage or identity is inferred.

## Compatibility and remaining evidence

The new original EPI123@1 and successor bank, source ledger, model, route, catalog, and localization bindings are versioned. Draft Spanish and Arabic remain unavailable; the public application remains English-only. Historical EP16 support from EPI104 and EPI105 is replayable under its original model but does not transfer automatically to this successor. Raw responses are unchanged.

Human response-process work is needed to learn whether respondents understand the “no conceivable difference” condition and whether EPI103 and EPI123 provide distinct evidence in practice. Real response data would also be needed to evaluate option use, local dependence, and threshold performance. The software regressions establish only the authored inference contract.
