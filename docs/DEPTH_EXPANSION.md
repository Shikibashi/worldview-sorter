# Depth expansion milestone

## Bank v0.5

Expansion batch 0.3 changes the objective from breadth to minimum subscale depth.

The prior bank had at least one candidate item somewhere for every headline and primary construct, but that was not enough to support even provisional multi-item measurement.

The strict audit therefore counts only **primary-role indicators**.

Secondary cross-loadings, ranking appearances, and derived interpretations do not satisfy the depth requirement.

## Result

The v0.5 bank contains **242 candidate items**.

Across the 122 headline and primary constructs:

- 121 constructs have exactly 2 primary-role indicators.
- 1 construct has 3 primary-role indicators.
- 0 constructs have fewer than 2 primary-role indicators.

CI now enforces this floor.

## What was added

Batch 0.3 adds 97 items.

The design deliberately mixes:

- natural counter-keyed propositions;
- second direct indicators;
- paired tradeoffs;
- diagnostic single-choice items;
- philosophical vignettes;
- direct value-importance items.

The purpose is not to create mechanically parallel wording. Multiple items for the same construct should approach that construct from meaningfully different manifestations or cases.

## Counter-keying

The expansion substantially improves agreement-item polarity balance.

Counter-keying is used when a natural statement exists. It is not generated by blindly adding "not" to every positive item.

This matters because awkward reverse wording can create a method factor of its own.

## Still not a scoring model

Two candidate indicators are a **development floor**, not a reliability claim.

The bank still needs:

1. cognitive review of exact item revisions;
2. expansion to three or more viable indicators for constructs intended as standalone latent scales;
3. pilot respondent data;
4. exploratory/confirmatory dimensionality analysis;
5. item discrimination and threshold estimation where appropriate;
6. local-dependence and method-factor checks;
7. reliability, test-retest, convergent/discriminant validity, DIF, and invariance work.

The v0.5 milestone exists so those later analyses begin from a bank where no public construct is represented by a single accidental wording.
