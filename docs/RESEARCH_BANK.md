# Candidate research bank v0.8

The project has reached the planned **472-item candidate research-bank size**.

## Coverage state

### Public-facing constructs

All 122 headline and primary constructs retain at least three standalone primary-role indicators.

### Diagnostic and research constructs

Every directly measured diagnostic/research construct meets the candidate-item target declared in `data/constructs.json`.

Derived constructs remain derived and have no direct primary item pool.

Examples include:

- metaethical family;
- identity-under-transformation interpretation;
- agency-theory family;
- response-to-meaninglessness interpretation.

These are computed from lower-level measurements rather than measured as independent latent variables.

## Final expansion batch

Expansion batch 0.6 adds 109 items:

- 89 items close the diagnostic/research candidate-target deficits;
- 20 items deepen broad public constructs where the earlier bank remained comparatively thin.

The bank now contains substantial coverage of:

- moral truth-aptness and moral motivation;
- rule-mediated ethics and intention/side-effect sensitivity;
- liberty/opposition-to-domination as an experimental moral foundation;
- theories of well-being and aesthetic value;
- external-world stance, justification theory, and values in science;
- laws of nature, causation, metaontology, time, and modality;
- consciousness theories and mental-content externalism;
- divinity models;
- objective-purpose sources and primal-world beliefs;
- just-world beliefs;
- legal positivism/nonpositivism;
- reform versus institutional rupture.

## Domain distribution

The final item count is 472, but the original per-domain targets are not treated as hard quotas.

Actual counts reflect the construct map:

- Metaethics: 26
- Normative ethics: 44
- Moral foundations: 28
- Values & axiology: 62
- Epistemology: 45
- Metaphysics & ontology: 32
- Mind & self: 23
- Agency & human nature: 36
- Religion & cosmology: 35
- Existential orientation: 24
- Social ontology & human relations: 40
- Political, legal & economic philosophy: 77

Political/legal/economic philosophy exceeds its original domain estimate because that domain ultimately contains 26 constructs. Values also exceeds its initial estimate because the refined values module contains many separately represented values.

The 472 total is therefore retained as the bank-size milestone while domain quotas remain descriptive planning estimates.

## What v0.8 is

It is:

- a broad candidate research bank;
- versioned and machine-readable;
- structurally heterogeneous;
- ready for pilot/scoring-model work.

It is not:

- a validated scale;
- a final public questionnaire;
- a validated short form;
- evidence that every provisional construct is empirically distinct;
- evidence that every item belongs in the eventual instrument.

## Next phase

The next logical phase is **pilot/scoring architecture**, not more item writing.

That phase should define:

1. anonymous raw-response collection format;
2. provisional keyed scoring used only for engineering/debugging;
3. calibration export suitable for later EFA/CFA/IRT work;
4. item-level missingness and response-time summaries;
5. model-versioned result snapshots;
6. short-form selection machinery that can later consume empirical item parameters.

The candidate bank should remain frozen at v0.8 except for specific bugfix revisions until pilot evidence justifies changes.
