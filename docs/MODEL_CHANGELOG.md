# Philosophical model changes

## Model release 1.0.0 — operational baseline

This manifest pins the existing authored pilot item bank, construct registry, interpretation model, route policy, affinity catalog, source records, localization bundles, and result-semantics label. It predates executable-source pinning, so its exact historical engine behavior cannot be proved from the manifest alone. It is not psychometric validation.

## Model release 1.1.0 — executable-source pin

[Release 1.1.0](../data/releases/model-release-v1.1.0.json) adds the immutable [engine-source 1.0.0 archive](../data/releases/engine-sources/engine-source-1.0.0/manifest.json). It hashes the route, response, interpretation, localization, and affinity modules used by new administrations. Its channel configuration was `channels-v2.json`; `channels-v1.json` remains available with the earlier complete bundle.

## Model release 1.2.0 — retire the conflated SO09 interpretation

[Release 1.2.0](../data/releases/model-release-v1.2.0.json) retires the public SO09 rule because `SOI004@2` asks whether an equal claim can occur while `SOI029@1` asks usual priority in one matched choice. The [released proposal](../data/governance/proposals/MCP-2026-007.json) and [evidence review](governance/MORAL_SCOPE_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md) record the philosophical and behavioral case. The direct public rule count changes from 141 to 140; SO09 becomes an explicit coverage gap. The associated prototype comparison and form opportunity bundle are removed. Quick, Standard, and Full keep the same 64/120/238 item IDs, revisions, and order, with new route/form versions. The affinity doctrines and translated wording do not change; their catalogs have new model-binding versions. Historical releases 1.0.0 and 1.1.0 remain byte-pinned. This release's channel configuration was `channels-v3.json`. No respondent answers were rewritten, and no psychometric inference is claimed.

## Model release 1.4.0 — scoped Easy Ontology comparison

[Release 1.4.0](../data/releases/model-release-v1.4.0.json) adds a seventh public doctrinal comparison for the limited `OM14` methodological proposition. The three existing `OMI109@1`, `OMI110@1`, and `OMI111@1` items support or oppose that proposition only on Full; Quick and Standard leave it unmeasured. Wider application of the method and its compatibility with realism remain explicitly unmeasured or disputed. Ordinary-object realism cannot substitute for the method. The [governed proposals](../data/governance/proposals/MCP-2026-011.json) and [regression](../scripts/test-easy-ontology-release.mjs) record sources and false-positive controls. The release pins [engine-source 1.2.0](../data/releases/engine-sources/engine-source-1.2.0/manifest.json) for the new `quiz-1.8.0` client version. The bank, 140 public direct rules, and 64/120/238 exact item assignments are unchanged; earlier release manifests remain available. `channels-v5.json` targets this release. This is an authored comparison, not psychometric validation.

## Model release 1.5.0 — protected federal division

[Release 1.5.0](../data/releases/model-release-v1.5.0.json) adds `PLI126@1` and `PLI127@1` to a versioned 240-item Full route, leaving the 64- and 120-item assignments intact. The new `PL34` direct rule asks about constitutionally protected powers at both national and regional levels. It needs two concordant direct evidence units; revocable delegation, unilateral regional control, generic localism, mixed answers, and route omission do not become support. The public direct-rule count is 141, of which 91 have a complete Full-route opportunity. The seven existing philosophical comparisons are re-bound without adding a tradition or treating federalism as an identity. The [content review](governance/FEDERAL_DIVISION_REVIEW.md), [governed proposals](../data/governance/proposals/MCP-2026-019.json), and [regression](../scripts/test-federal-content-release.mjs) record the scope. The release pins [engine-source 1.3.0](../data/releases/engine-sources/engine-source-1.3.0/manifest.json) and `channels-v6.json`; earlier releases and item revisions remain available.

## Model release 1.6.0 — two scoped epistemic comparisons

[Release 1.6.0](../data/releases/model-release-v1.6.0.json) adds fallible knowledge and experience grounded external-world knowledge to a nine-entry philosophical comparison catalog. The existing `EP15` and `EP20` direct rules receive exact propositions and rule-linked claims from [IEP Fallibilism](https://iep.utm.edu/fallibil/) and [SEP Rationalism vs. Empiricism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/rationalism-empiricism/). No item revision, answer mapping, route question, or public result-state threshold changes. Quick leaves both new defining criteria unmeasured; Standard can assess only fallible knowledge; Full can assess both. The [scoped review](governance/EPISTEMIC_AFFINITY_REVIEW.md), [approved proposals](../data/governance/proposals/MCP-2026-029.json), and [regression](../scripts/test-epistemic-affinity-release.mjs) record the false-positive boundaries. New client archive 1.4.0 and `channels-v7.json` preserve historical replay. These are authored comparisons, not identities or empirical validation.

## Model release 1.7.0 — direct act-consequence criterion

[Release 1.7.0](../data/releases/model-release-v1.7.0.json) adds `NEI122@1` and the unchanged existing `NEI014@1` to a successor 242-item Full route. The new `NE22` public proposition distinguishes a direct maximizing act-outcome criterion from consequence-justified rules, independent duties, and generic concern for outcomes. Two concordant authored evidence units are required for support or opposition; one unit is labeled a lean, conflict is mixed, and route omission is `not_measured`. Quick and Standard retain their 64/120 exact question revisions. There are now 565 bank items, 142 public direct rules, and 92 Full-route directional opportunities; 50 public direct rules still lack sufficient Full-route content. The nine philosophical comparisons remain unchanged in doctrine and gain no new mapping. The [content review](governance/ACT_CONSEQUENCE_REVIEW.md), [governed proposals](../data/governance/proposals/MCP-2026-043.json), and [regression](../scripts/test-act-consequence-content-release.mjs) record sources, boundaries, and false-positive cases. The release pins [engine-source 1.5.0](../data/releases/engine-sources/engine-source-1.5.0/manifest.json) and `channels-v8.json`; older releases remain available for exact replay. This is authored content, not empirical validation.
